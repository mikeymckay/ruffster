Questions, answered plainly

Clear answers for your family's Ruffster report.

Ruffster helps families understand computer use together, especially during school hours. Here’s what to expect when you try the Mac app together.

Is Ruffster ready to install on my child's computer?

Yes. The Mac app is ready to try together and is designed for ongoing transparent use. macOS will ask you to confirm before opening it because Ruffster is not signed by Apple. The guide explains the first step, and Windows setup is being prepared for a family-computer try. See the getting-started guide .

Why does my Mac ask me to confirm the first time?

Ruffster is an open-source app that has not yet been signed by Apple. If your Mac cannot verify Ruffster, you can approve Ruffster for this Mac in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, then open it again and choose Open. This is an exception for Ruffster only; it leaves your Mac’s general app protections on. If macOS says the app will damage your computer or is damaged, stop and contact the project. The getting-started guide has the steps and Apple’s instructions.

When does Ruffster run?

On a child’s Mac, Ruffster starts automatically after that account signs in. macOS relaunches the background agent if it stops unexpectedly. The menu-bar paw shows whether monitoring is starting, has recent samples, or is paused. Ruffster does not collect activity at the login screen or before the child signs in.

What does it collect?

Ruffster records the foreground app, total network traffic, and simple DNS, proxy, and VPN indicators. Mac setup also offers an optional estimate of network traffic by app or service. Website names are a separate optional browser experiment and are not included in the Mac setup.

What stays private?

Ruffster records app names and basic network totals so families can see how a computer is used. Screenshots, keystrokes, audio, camera data, message contents, and full website addresses stay out of Ruffster. The app keeps its status visible and does not block apps or websites.

Do I need a browser add-on, and whose computer is it for?

No. Ruffster works without an add-on. A separate browser experiment can show domain names from selected browsers on the child's computer. The child can pause it or use another browser, and the report makes clear when website details were not observed. This experiment is not part of Mac setup yet.

Can it tell how much data a particular website used?

Not yet. Ruffster reports total traffic for each network connection. It can estimate how much happened during the school schedule you choose, but it cannot say how much traffic a particular website used.

What do the school-hours and network-label estimates mean?

You choose school days and hours. Ruffster uses that schedule to estimate how much recorded network traffic happened during those hours. You can also label familiar networks Home or School. These views are helpful context, but they do not confirm the computer's location and some totals may overlap.

Does a VPN or DNS indicator prove that filtering is working?

Ruffster shows a few settings the computer reports; it cannot confirm that a filter is working. A filter set up only on your home router stays on that network. A device app or profile may work on school Wi-Fi or a hotspot too. Check your provider on each network, and ask the school before changing school-managed settings. NextDNS explains router and device setup as one example.

Is my child's information uploaded to a cloud service?

No cloud account is needed. To connect the computers, the sender shows two digits and the receiver enters them. Both computers need to be on the same Wi-Fi; the digits help them find each other, while reports remain encrypted for the parent. If devices cannot find each other on a particular network, save the encrypted report file on the child's computer and open it in the parent dashboard. The report includes new observations, skips exact repeats, and follows the parent's retention setting. The dashboard can name each computer and show its report separately. On Mac, allow Local Network access when asked so Ruffster can find the parent computer for setup and report sharing.

Can I use one parent report for more than one child computer?

Yes. The dashboard keeps each computer's activity in its own view, and you can give each one a name. Those names are saved in your encrypted parent report. We’re now trying report sharing between family Macs and preparing the Windows family flow.

Who holds the encryption key?

Choose six numbers your child does not know. Your PIN opens the parent dashboard, and reports are encrypted for the parent computer. Save the recovery copy before setup. If you move to a new parent computer, choose “Restore parent access,” select the .vault file, and enter the same PIN. Older recovery copies still use the phrase you chose at the time. Keep the PIN and recovery copy somewhere you can find them so your family can keep its reports.

What does a gap in the report mean?

Ruffster marks the time when it has no observations to add. A computer may be asleep, updating, or simply offline; the report leaves the reason open. The status indicator and timeline help your family see which parts of the day are covered.

Does the school need to set anything up?

No school installation or administration is intended. Families choose a school-hours schedule themselves. That schedule creates a time-based report and does not prove that the computer was at school.

Which computers are priorities?

macOS comes first, then Windows. Linux is used as a development and test host only; there are no plans for students to use the Linux version in the current scope.

I tried an earlier developer build. What about its files?

Files created by early developer builds may not use Ruffster’s current encrypted report format. The current setup does not change older files automatically. If you tried an earlier developer build, keep using its existing report there and ask for help before bringing those files into the family app.

How long are reports kept, and can they be deleted?

You can choose how long to keep detail on the child computer and parent copy. The parent dashboard can remove one computer from a combined report or delete the whole local parent copy. Those actions leave the child's copy and previously saved report files in place. You can delete those separately from where they were saved.

How are European privacy rules handled?

Privacy rights depend on your country and family situation. We are continuing a privacy review as Ruffster grows; this page is not legal advice.
